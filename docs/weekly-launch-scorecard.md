## Interpretation rules

- Optimize for completed registrations and successful first journeys rather
  than raw impressions or clicks.
- Do not rank communities or people by inferred kink interests, risk or value.
- Small counts can be identifying. Suppress or combine breakdowns when a cell
  has fewer than ten events.
- Treat attribution as directional: first-touch data is limited to the current
  browser tab and analytics remains consent-led.
- Do not enable paid promotion merely because organic reach is low; promotion
  remains behind the product, safety, policy and owner go-live gates.
