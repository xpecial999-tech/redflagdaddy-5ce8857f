# RedFlagDaddy organic content tracker

Updated: 28 August 2026

Nothing in this tracker is approved for publication. Update the approval fields
only after the product go-live decision, a fresh platform/community-rule check,
and final review of the exact copy, visual, alt text and destination link.

## Status key

- `Drafted`: copy exists but has not been approved.
- `Needs visual`: copy exists; final brand artwork is not prepared.
- `Ready for approval`: copy, visual, accessibility and link fields are complete.
- `Approved`: owner has approved the exact final package and publication time.
- `Published`: live; record the URL and results without collecting sensitive
  audience data.
- `Stopped`: paused because of rules, moderation feedback, safety concerns or
  poor-quality engagement.

## Measurement boundary

Measure aggregate reach, meaningful replies, consented site visits, completed
sign-ups and successful first journeys. Do not import usernames, profile text,
private messages, community membership, inferred kink interests or individual
engagement histories into RedFlagDaddy analytics or the tracker.
