# RedFlagDaddy four-week organic launch plan

Updated: 28 August 2026

**Status:** Content preparation only. Do not publish or promote until the
go-live checklist is approved and construction mode is intentionally disabled.

## Positioning to use everywhere

- Audience: adults 18+ in kink communities, starting with FetLife and adjacent
  consent/relationship communities.
- Primary action: **Create an account** at `https://redflagdaddy.com/`.
- Promise: structured, role-aware prompts that help two adults discuss consent,
  compatibility, safety practices and possible red flags more clearly.
- Boundary: RedFlagDaddy is not identity verification, a background check,
  diagnosis, proof of consent, an emergency service or a guarantee of safety.
- Voice: fun, clear, non-political and never flippant about consent or risk.
- Never publish user results, screenshots, testimonials, private messages or
  identifiable stories without specific written permission for that exact use.

## Channel rules

### FetLife

- Participate as a genuine community member before mentioning the product.
- Ask group leaders before posting a product link or commercially adjacent
  discussion. FetLife may pre-moderate discussions that contain external links
  or potential commercial promotion.
- Never create a fake or rolling event to advertise the app. FetLife's event
  rules exclude online-service advertisements and events designed to solicit
  off-site followers.
- Keep private conversations private and never publish phone numbers, journey
  details or other members' information.
- If paid promotion is later considered, contact FetLife through its official
  advertising channel instead of improvising sponsored posts.

### Reddit

- Read each community's rules and ask moderators before linking to the product.
- Keep at least nine contributions helpful and non-promotional for every direct
  product post. This is a working safety ratio, not permission to ignore a
  community that prohibits promotion entirely.
- Do not cross-post the same promotion repeatedly, mass-tag people, send
  unsolicited chats or use multiple accounts to amplify posts.
- Prefer complete native answers. Add a RedFlagDaddy link only where it is
  directly relevant and permitted.

### X

- Use educational, text-led and brand-safe posts. Explicit media is unnecessary
  for this product and should not be used in profile, header or promotional
  creative.
- Never share intimate media or identifiable personal information. Any future
  adult content must follow X's sensitive-media controls, but the recommended
  RedFlagDaddy launch feed remains non-explicit.

### Instagram, Facebook and paid ads

- Hold paid campaigns and product-specific Meta promotion until the exact
  creative, landing page, targeting and then-current platform policy have been
  reviewed. Do not attempt to evade adult-content or targeting restrictions.
- Reusable educational posts may be prepared in a non-explicit form, but they
  still require a final policy review immediately before publication.

## Four-week calendar

Publish three substantive pieces per week. Reformat one idea per channel rather
than pasting identical text everywhere. Comments and helpful replies are part of
the plan; unsolicited direct messages are not.

### Week 1 — Listen and establish usefulness

1. **Discussion:** “What makes a pre-dynamic conversation feel useful instead
   of clinical?” Ask for general principles only; explicitly request no private
   stories, names or screenshots.
2. **Educational post:** a five-question check-in covering current boundaries,
   pause signals, privacy, aftercare and what would make either person stop.
3. **Founder note:** explain why RedFlagDaddy is being built as a conversation
   aid, followed by the full product boundary. No product link unless the group
   allows it.

Goal: learn the community's language and collect themes, not user data. Record
only non-identifying editorial observations.

### Week 2 — Demonstrate the method

1. **Carousel/thread:** “Hard limit, soft limit, curious, not discussed.” Show
   why “not discussed” is different from agreement.
2. **Prompt of the week:** “If either of us goes quiet, freezes or cannot use a
   safeword, what should happen?” Reinforce that a safeword does not replace
   ongoing attention or consent.
3. **Privacy explainer:** what the app intentionally cannot prove, what users
   should never put into a public post, and why private links must be treated as
   sensitive.

Goal: saves, thoughtful replies and consent-quality feedback—not reach alone.

### Week 3 — Show the product honestly

1. **Synthetic walkthrough:** use only the clearly labelled demo report. Show
   the flow from role-aware prompts to conversation topics without implying a
   diagnosis or safety score guarantee.
2. **Feature post:** private Markdown, topics-only, JSON and discreet calendar
   exports. Explain that these files are generated on the user's device and
   should still be stored carefully.
3. **Access post:** account creation is the recommended starting point; guest
   mode exists for people who prefer no notification details. Explain the owner
   code's loss and expiry consequences plainly.

Goal: identify confusing copy and objections before launch. Link only in places
where moderators or platform rules allow it.

### Week 4 — Controlled launch

Publish only after the go/no-go record is complete.

1. **Launch post:** one clear problem, one honest product promise, the 18+
   boundary and one `Create an account` link.
2. **How it works:** create a journey, send the private partner link, complete
   separately, then use the results as prompts for a real conversation.
3. **Feedback invitation:** request usability and clarity feedback through the
   approved support form. Explain that it is not continuously monitored or an
   emergency/crisis service. It may receive privacy, exposed-link and safety
   reports, but must not solicit intimate details.

Goal: completed account creation and successful first journeys, not raw clicks.

## Daily community routine

- Spend more time answering relevant questions than posting links.
- Check each group's rules before every post; rules can change.
- Reply without diagnosing relationships or declaring someone safe/unsafe.
- Move product-support issues to the approved support channel, never a personal
  account or public thread.
- Remove or redact accidentally shared personal information promptly and follow
  the incident process once approved.
- Keep a simple publication log: channel, community, post URL, permission or
  rule checked, date, topic and non-sensitive outcome.

## Measurement and stop conditions

Use only the approved first-party funnel events with 35-day retention:
landing-page view, signup started, signup completed and first journey created.
Do not import FetLife/Reddit identities, infer kink attributes, build cross-site
profiles or upload community member lists.

Pause a channel immediately if posts are removed as promotion, moderators ask
for a stop, users report privacy confusion, support capacity is exceeded, or a
launch-critical defect appears. Construction mode remains the product-wide
pause control.
